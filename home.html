About Me

Hello, I am Pin-Chieh Huang (TMWF), a Master's student in Computer Science at Tunghai University.

I am passionate about cybersecurity, AI applications, and building practical systems that solve real-world problems. I enjoy turning ideas into working systems and continuously exploring new technologies to improve their practicality and impact.

My work focuses on areas such as threat intelligence, vulnerability analysis, and AI-assisted automation. I have experience working with technologies like large language models (LLMs), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and cloud platforms.

I have worked as a Threat Strategy Analysis Intern at WNC, where I developed an AI-based threat intelligence system using OpenCTI, integrating multiple data sources to support automated analysis, correlation, and risk assessment, significantly improving efficiency and reducing manual workload.

In addition to industry experience, I have participated in research projects related to LLM-based analysis and model reliability, focusing on how AI can be applied to security scenarios and complex data interpretation.

I also have hands-on experience in a variety of system development projects, including cloud architecture solutions, mobile applications, and data-driven systems. Some of my projects include building an educational game for cybersecurity awareness, developing a factory production management system, and conducting public sentiment analysis using machine learning techniques.

Through these experiences, I have developed a strong interest in designing systems that combine AI, software engineering, and cybersecurity to create practical and scalable solutions.

This website is a place where I share my projects, technical work, and things I am currently exploring.
